About Us

A new day calls for a fresh start, and that starts with clean clothes. Laundry Corner is a family-owned business that offers affordable prices and convenient services, open from 8:00 AM to 6:00 PM, Monday through Sunday. We are dedicated to providing quality service that is accessible and welcoming to everyone. We hope to make every laundry day a little easier, so you can step into a new day feeling refreshed and ready for whatever good things lie ahead.

Our Values

Affordability

We believe that quality laundry services should be accessible to everyone at reasonable prices.

Quality Service

We strive to provide dependable laundry care and ensure that every customer receives service they can count on.

Convenience

We aim to make laundry day easier with accessible services and daily operating hours that fit into our customers' routines.

Customer Care

We treat every customer with kindness and respect, creating a welcoming experience with every visit.

Community

As a family-owned business, we value our place in the community and strive to make a positive difference in the everyday lives of the people we serve.

Social Awareness

We strive to minimize plastic waste by using reusable, custom-designed laundry bags, reuse suitable customer-provided bags, and practice proper waste segregation to promote a cleaner, more sustainable environment.
