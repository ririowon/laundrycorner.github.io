Do I need to bring my own detergent and fabric conditioner?

No need to worry! Detergent and fabric conditioner are already included with every wash. However, you are also welcome to bring your own if you prefer.

Can I leave my laundry at the shop and come back for it later?

Absolutely! You can either pick up your finished laundry directly from our shop or arrange to have it delivered to your preferred location. Just let our staff know your preferred option when dropping off your laundry!

How do you handle delicate clothing or garments that require special care?

Please inform our staff of any special washing instructions before leaving your laundry so we can discuss the appropriate care for your garments.

What should I do if an item is missing or damaged after laundry service?

Please inform our staff as soon as possible so we can look into the concern and assist you accordingly.

Should I separate my white clothes from colored clothes before bringing them in?

Yes, separating whites from colored garments is recommended to help prevent color transfer and keep your clothes looking their best. You may also inform our staff of any specific laundry preferences.
